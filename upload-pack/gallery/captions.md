# Gallery captions

Upload in numbered order and set **01** as featured.

1. **One click empties your inventory**
   Press the button next to the chest and every unlocked slot empties into it. The sword, pickaxe, torches and food were locked, so they stayed behind.

2. **Lock the slots you keep**
   Hold Left Alt and click any inventory or hotbar slot to lock it. Locked slots are tinted blue and Safe Deposit never touches them. Click again to unlock.

3. **Locks stay with you**
   Locks belong to the slot, not the item, so whatever you keep in that slot stays protected. They show in your normal inventory too and are saved between sessions.

4. **Chests, barrels and shulker boxes**
   The button shows up on chests, double chests, barrels, ender chests and shulker boxes. A shulker box never gets another shulker box pushed into it.

5. **A full chest is fine**
   Safe Deposit fills what space there is and stops. Anything that doesn't fit stays where it was, so nothing ends up on the floor.
