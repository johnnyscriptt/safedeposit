## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-09

### Added
- A Safe Deposit button next to chests, double chests, barrels, ender chests and shulker boxes. One click moves everything from your inventory into the container.
- Lock any inventory or hotbar slot by holding Left Alt and clicking it. Locked slots are tinted blue and are never deposited.
- Locks are saved, and the lock key can be changed in Options, Controls, Safe Deposit.
- Sits under Stardew Chest Buttons' buttons when both mods are installed.

### Known limitations
- Needs the mod on the server too. On a server without it, the button is greyed out.
- Locks only affect Safe Deposit. Shift-click, dropping and other sorting mods ignore them.
- Not available in the creative inventory screen.
